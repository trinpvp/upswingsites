Before you come

Arrive ready. It shapes the cut.

Your hair is cut dry and in its natural pattern, so how it looks when you walk in is what your stylist works with.

  1. Hair down, fully dryWorn loose in its natural pattern.
  2. Washed the same dayFor the best result, and out of consideration for your stylist.
  3. No ponytails, clips or hat headNothing that bends your curls out of shape on the way in.
  4. Minimal product, no brushingDon't brush styling products in, and skip the Denman brush.
  5. Completely at a loss?Wash, condition and air dry. That's it.

Coming in unprepared can mean your appointment can't go ahead, so please read this before you book.

Arrive styled as discussed

Express cuts and existing clients: come in styled the way you and your stylist talked about at your last appointment. Express Curly Cuts are ideal within 6 months of your previous visit.

Good questions

Before you ask.

Quick answers before you book. Service details live in the booking menu.

Do I need a “curly” haircut?
Curly cuts are for anyone who wears their hair in its natural state most of the time, from tight coils to loose waves. Your hair is cut dry, in its own pattern, and finished in your natural texture.
Do you cut kids' hair?
Yes, as long as they can sit still through the appointment. Alejandra, Alexis, Aisha and Coral see kids under 13. Kids book as new clients, unless a parent is an existing client who knows how to prep for an express cut.
Do you cut men's hair?
Of course. Book a new-client curly cut for your first visit.
Do I have to book a color consultation?
Nope. Only if you might need a color correction, and so a test strand. Your stylist may reach out before your appointment to make sure enough time is booked.
What's an apprentice appointment?
It's how newer stylists train. Apprentices have finished a year of cosmetology school, passed their licensing exams and completed 6 months of in-house training. Services are discounted, and may take a little more time and instruction.
Something not right after a visit?
Let the salon know within two weeks of your appointment through the contact form on curlcraftsalon.com.
