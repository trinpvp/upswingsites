Our story

A family cafe, since 1995

Donny's started as a family business in 1995, and it's still family-owned today. We pour organic, fair-trade coffee and tea, we've worked with Cafe Moto for more than 25 years, and our breads and pastries come in from Bread & Cie.

Good coffee matters to us. Our neighbors matter more: serving this community with the love of Christ is the heart of everything we do.
