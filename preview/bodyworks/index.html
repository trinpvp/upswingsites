What we offer

Three pillars, one plan built around you.

Massage is the heart of the clinic. Red light therapy, acupuncture and microneedling sit alongside it, so your care can draw on more than one approach.

Pillar 01

With Joseph Christodoulos, Certified Medical Massage Therapist

Therapeutic massage

Our core service is neuromuscular (medical) massage: focused, deep-tissue work on muscle tension and chronic pain. It starts with a thorough assessment, so the session is aimed at what you came in for.

  • Assessment first, then treatment planned around you
  • Many medical insurance plans cover neuromuscular massage
  • Gentler options too, from recovery massage to reflexology

Pillar 02

Also known as photobiomodulation

Red light therapy

Red light therapy (RLT) uses specific wavelengths of red and near-infrared light. It is non-invasive and uses no ultraviolet light. Each session is set up around your goals, whether that is recovery, relaxation or skin care.

  • Non-invasive, with no UV light
  • Customized to your goals, session by session
  • Can be combined with massage and lymphatic drainage

Planning your sessions

For longer-term goals the clinic usually suggests a series of sessions. Call and we'll help you work out what fits.

Pillar 03

With Jordi Capdet, Licensed Acupuncturist

Acupuncture & microneedling

Traditional acupuncture, cosmetic (facial) acupuncture and microneedling, also called collagen induction therapy. Microneedling is our core skin service; your plan depends on your goals and skin type, and starts with a skin and health consultation.

  • Consultation first: a full skin and health assessment
  • Numbing if needed, precise depth control, soothing serums and aftercare guidance
  • Minor redness or sensitivity may last 24–48 hours after microneedling
