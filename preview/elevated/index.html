The Elevated way

Dessert, minus the compromise.

We choose every ingredient with intention, go easy on the natural sweeteners and bake by hand each day. The goal is simple: make eating vegan and gluten-free fun and genuinely delicious.

Our story

It started with one cheesecake for Karina.

When Tanya's sister Karina developed a dairy allergy at 22, she spent years searching for something to replace the desserts she loved. Nothing came close. So Tanya, now Elevated's owner and founder, set out to surprise her with her favorite dessert: a cheesecake made completely dairy-free.

Karina's face lit up at the first bite, and a few happy tears followed: real cheesecake again, without the pain. That was the moment Elevated was born.

Care + community
