Inside the studio

Real machines. Real skills. Their own ideas.

Every student works on a project matched to their skill level, from first stitches to full garments. Alongside painting and drawing, kids pick up the craft behind the clothes:

  • Machine threading & care
  • Pattern making
  • Hand embroidery & needlework
  • Creative problem-solving

What comes home: clothes, bags, plush and gifts.

Parties & celebrations

Every guest makes something.

Paint or sew parties for kids' birthdays, team building, and baby and bridal showers. The guest of honor picks the theme, the studio handles the lesson and the décor, and everyone heads home with something they made.

How a party works

  1. 1Arrive 30 minutes early to set up. The party table is yours for food, drinks and gifts.
  2. 2A 1.5-hour painting or sewing lesson, themed by the guest of honor.
  3. 3Then it's time for cake, with 30 minutes for clean-up afterwards.

Meet the teacher

Meet Ms. Jaimee.

After UC San Diego and FIDM in Los Angeles, Jaimee spent 13 years as a clothing and swimwear designer in Orange County's surf industry, until San Diego called her home. She has taught children's fine art, arts and crafts camps, adult art, sketching, sewing and needle-craft for 25 years.

Her own story started early: painting animal portraits in an outdoor class as a kid in Northern California, then summer sewing camp with a local sew-guru. Paint Box is her way of giving kids that same warm, encouraging start with art.

  • 13 yrsdesigning clothing & swimwear
  • 25 yrsteaching art & sewing
  • UCSDthen FIDM, Los Angeles
Why "Paint Box"?

The studio is named for Jaimee's favorite childhood book, Panda Bear's Paint Box, illustrated by Christopher Santoro. She'd stare at its bright, shiny paint for hours.
