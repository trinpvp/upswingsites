La nostra storia

Not just Italian. Napoletano.

Italian food is easy to find in San Diego. The cooking of Naples, the city where pizza was born and the sea shapes the menu, is much harder to come by. Siamo Napoli has been bringing it to North Park since September 2019.

Italian Chef Alfonso cooks with the best ingredients and real attention to detail. The pasta starts with excellent flour, the pizza goes into a blue-tiled oven, and the dining room pairs classic Italian style with a modern twist, so 30th Street can feel a lot like Naples.

Pizza napoletana

Classic rosse and white pizze bianche, from the Margherita to the truffle Tartufata.

Pasta fresca

Handmade ravioli and scialatelli, gnocchi, and Nonna's lasagna.

Frutti di mare

Carlsbad mussels, calamari, clams, branzino and a Positano cioppino.

Ask your server about the Pizza dello Chef and today's soup.
