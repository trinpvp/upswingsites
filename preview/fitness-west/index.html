Our story

Family-run.
PB-rooted.

Brian has been part of the Fitness West family since 1989, and he always knew that if he ever owned a gym, it would be this one. In January 2018 that goal came true when he and his wife, Shari, took over the gym.

Shari is a second-generation San Diego native who grew up in Del Mar riding and showing horses. Together they love Pacific Beach and stay deeply involved in the neighborhood. In 2023 Brian’s commitment to giving back was recognized when he was named Honorary Mayor of Pacific Beach.
