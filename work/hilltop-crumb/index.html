Our story

A small bakery with a very old starter.

Hilltop Crumb began with one stone-deck oven, a handful of early risers and a jar of starter passed down from a grandmother’s kitchen. We still mix, shape and bake everything by hand, right here in Escondido.

Our flour is stone-milled in California, our butter is cultured, and our fruit comes from farms close enough to visit. No shortcuts and nothing par-baked. Just patience and a very early alarm.
