    07FAQ

    Good questions.

    Still wondering about something? Text us at (760) 555-0127 and a real detailer will answer.

    Do you need my water or power?

    No. The van carries filtered water, a quiet generator and its own lighting. We just need about four feet of space around the car.

    Can you detail at my office or apartment?

    Usually, yes. Office lots, condo garages and driveways all work. If your HOA or building has rules, tell us and we’ll plan around them.

    What happens if it rains?

    We check the forecast and text you the day before. Light drizzle is fine under our canopy. For real rain we reschedule at no charge.

    How long does a ceramic coating last?

    Ceramic Shield is rated for up to five years with simple maintenance washes. Every coating comes with an aftercare kit and a 30-day check-in.
